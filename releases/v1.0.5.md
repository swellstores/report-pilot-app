# Report Pilot 1.0.5

## New

- **Send at:** choose the hour reports are sent, next to the Time zone setting. It defaults to 08:00, so nothing changes until you pick another time.
- **Send only to:** in Actions → Send a report now, enter one address to send a test of a report to just that address.

## More accurate figures

- **Sales:** shipping, refunds and shipping discounts are counted correctly, and draft orders are left out.
- **Subscriptions:** a subscription set to cancel or pause when its billing period ends still counts as active, but no longer adds to monthly recurring revenue or Renewals in the next 7 days.
- **Abandoned carts:** a cart holding two variants of the same product counts as one cart for that product.
- **Discounts:** each order counts once per coupon or promotion.
- **Inventory:** archived variants are left out, and Est. days left uses the last 30 whole days in the store's time zone.
- **Time zone:** each report's period and send time follow the app's Time zone setting.

## Emails

- Reports are built and sent in the background, so large stores no longer time out.
- If Swell refuses a read while a report is built, the send stops and shows as failed instead of emailing a report of zeros.
- Subjects show the report name and period, such as "Sales report: Oct 5 – Oct 11, 2026".
- Inbox previews show the intro line instead of repeating the date.
- A thin indigo line runs across the top of each email.
- Numbers and percentages use thousands separators, like the dashboard.
- "N more on this report in your dashboard" links straight to the report.
- The footer tells recipients outside the business to ask a store admin to change or stop their emails.
- Customers without a name appear as "Unnamed customer". Email addresses never appear in a report.

## Dashboard

- Report lists show 0 instead of a dash, and use thousands separators.
- Best sellers' share shows how many products it covers, such as "10 of 37 products".
- "View product" links always open the product in the Swell dashboard.
- Help text and descriptions are clearer throughout.
