Report Pilot turns your store data into reports your team actually reads.

Every morning, week, two weeks, or month — you choose — the app aggregates the orders in your reporting window and emails a clean, readable summary to the addresses you choose. No dashboard to remember to check, no spreadsheet to export, no third-party analytics account to provision.

## What's in every report

- Revenue, with the full breakdown: subtotal, discounts, shipping, and tax
- Order count, items sold, and average order value
- New versus returning customers, so you can see whether growth is coming from acquisition or repeat business
- Top products ranked by revenue, not just units, so a high-margin seller isn't buried under a cheap one
- A day-by-day table of orders and revenue, zero-filled so quiet days are visible rather than missing
- Revenue and order count measured against the previous period of equal length, so the headline figures show direction and not just totals
- Cancelled and unpaid orders reported separately, never silently folded into revenue

## More than sales

Turn on any of six more reports, each on its own daily, weekly, two-weekly, or monthly schedule and delivered as its own email:

- **Inventory** — what is out of stock or running low, and how many days the remaining stock will last at the recent rate of sale
- **Customers** — new versus returning buyers, repeat-purchase rate, and your top customers by spend
- **Discounts** — how much was discounted, which coupons and promotions were used, and the revenue those orders brought in
- **Products** — best and slowest sellers, the biggest movers against the previous period, and products that didn't sell at all
- **Abandoned carts** — how many carts were left without checking out, how many came back as orders, and the products most often left behind
- **Subscriptions** — active subscriptions and monthly recurring revenue, new and canceled subscriptions, failed renewals, and what renews in the next week

Need one right now? Send any report on demand from the app settings.

## Accurate by default

Revenue counts only orders that are paid and not cancelled. Reporting windows end at midnight UTC, so a report never includes a partial day and totals don't shift if you read the same report twice. Cancelled and unpaid orders are surfaced alongside the totals rather than quietly dropped, so the numbers reconcile.

## Built on Swell, not bolted on

Totals are aggregated server-side through Swell's Backend API rather than pulled down order by order, so reports stay fast as your volume grows. Each report is stored as a record in your store, giving you a browsable history under Reporting → Sales reports in the dashboard. Open any past period to see its full detail.

Because the email is driven by a standard Swell notification, you can edit the copy and subject line from the dashboard without touching code or waiting on a developer. Recipients are managed in the app settings.

## Configure it once

Pick daily, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. Choose who receives the emails. Choose how many top products to list. Turn the daily breakdown on or off. Decide whether quiet periods with no orders should still send — off by default, so an empty week stays quiet.

Read-only where it counts: the app requests read access to your orders, products, customers, carts, subscriptions, and settings. The only things it writes are its own report records and the notifications that deliver them to you.
