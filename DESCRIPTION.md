Report Pilot emails your store's key numbers to the people who need them, on a schedule, so nobody has to open the dashboard to know how the store is doing. Every report is also saved under Reporting in your dashboard, so you can open any past period again.

Choose any of seven reports, how often each one runs, and who gets it. Reports are sent at the hour and in the time zone you choose.

- **Sales and customers.** Revenue, orders and average order value against the period before, new and returning customers, top products by revenue and a day-by-day breakdown, plus repeat buyers and your top customers by spend.
- **Inventory and products.** What's out of stock or running low and how many days it will last at the last 30 days' rate of sale, best and slowest sellers, the biggest movers since the last period, and active products that didn't sell.
- **Discounts.** How much was given away, through which coupons and promotions, and what those orders brought in.
- **Abandoned carts and subscriptions.** Carts left without checking out, how many came back as orders and the products with the most value left behind, plus active subscriptions, monthly recurring revenue, new and canceled subscriptions, failed renewals and what renews in the next 7 days.
- **Your schedule, your people.** Daily, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, set for each report. One list gets every report that's on, anyone can be added to a single report, and any report can be sent right away from the Actions menu, to its recipients or to one test address.
- **Totals you can check.** Sales revenue counts paid orders that weren't canceled, less refunds. Canceled orders, unpaid orders and refunds are shown separately.

Setup takes a couple of minutes. In the app settings, add your recipients, choose your time zone and send time, and switch on the reports you want. The sales report is on from the start, and no other account or key is needed.
