Report Pilot emails your store's key numbers to the people who need them, on a schedule, so nobody has to open the dashboard to know how the store is doing. Every report is also saved in your dashboard, so you can open any past period again.

Choose any of seven reports, how often each one runs, and who gets it. Reports arrive around 08:00 in your store's time zone.

- **Sales.** Revenue, orders and average order value against the period before, new and returning customers, top products by revenue, and a day-by-day breakdown.
- **Customers.** Who bought, how many were new or came back, repeat buyers, and your top customers by spend.
- **Inventory.** What's out of stock or running low, and how many days the rest will last at the last 30 days' rate of sale.
- **Products.** Best and slowest sellers, the biggest movers since the last period, and products that didn't sell at all.
- **Discounts.** How much was given away, through which coupons and promotions, and what those orders brought in.
- **Abandoned carts.** Carts left without checking out, how many came back as orders, and the products most often left behind.
- **Subscriptions.** Active subscriptions and monthly recurring revenue, new and canceled subscriptions, failed renewals, and what renews in the next week.
- **Your schedule, your people.** Daily, weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, set for each report. One list gets every report, anyone can be added to a single report, and any report can be sent straight away from the Actions menu.
- **Totals you can trust.** Revenue counts only paid orders that weren't cancelled. Cancelled and unpaid orders are shown separately, never quietly dropped.

Setup takes a couple of minutes: add your recipients and time zone, then switch on the reports you want.
